# Change reversal and recovery boundary

This sanitized baseline has no deployment history, runtime archive, rollback
artifact, recovery procedure or authority to act on an environment. No command
here should be treated as a recovery instruction.

Before deploying any derivative, create a separately reviewed rollback and
recovery plan that identifies reversible scope, protected evidence custody,
backup prerequisites, restore validation, stop conditions and owner approval.
Do not delete, reuse, repack or overwrite unknown deployment evidence.
